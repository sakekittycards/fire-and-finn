# Google Business Profile posts (rebrand, first weeks)

Three posts, each an image from `posts.html` (1200 × 900, rendered to the PNG named here) and the text below. Text is under 300 characters, plain, no hashtags, no emoji, no claim beyond what the site and the signage already say. Nothing here is posted by anyone but the owner. Post them in this order, a few days apart; the rename post first.

Render the images with:

```
msedge --headless=new --disable-gpu --hide-scrollbars --virtual-time-budget=4000 --window-size=1200,900 --screenshot=rename.png "file:///.../menu-source/gbp/posts.html?post=rename"
```

(`?post=hero`, `?post=order` for the other two.)

## Rules

- Image text stays inside the central 80 % of the frame; Google crops to 4:3 and rounds the corners.
- Fonts and colours are the site's and the slides': Jost, ground `#0B0C0E`, ink `#F2F0EB`, fire `#F07840`, sea `#62C6EC`.
- No QR codes, no prices in the images (prices live on the boards and the menu page), no "same family" or sourcing claims.
- Retire the rename post from the profile the day the permanent sign goes up; the other two can stay.
