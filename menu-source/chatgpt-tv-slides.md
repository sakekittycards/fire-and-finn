# Fire & Finn — TV slides to regenerate (one prompt per slide)

The nine Island Panda TV slides need Fire & Finn versions. Paste **one prompt at a time** into ChatGPT, ideally a fresh chat per slide, and iterate on that single image until it's right. Never bundle two slides in one request.

Two slides are NOT in this file on purpose: the **menu board** and the **hours board**. Those carry exact prices and times, which an image generator will get wrong, so Claude builds them from the real menu as pixel-exact 1920×1080 images.

Shared look for every slide below (each prompt repeats it, so each one stands alone):

- Canvas 1920 × 1080, landscape, 16:9, edge-to-edge, no border.
- Background: near-black charcoal #080808 with a soft warm glow (#F07840 at low opacity) on the left and a soft cool blue glow (#36A9CF) on the right. Fine film grain. Never a bright beach cartoon.
- Accents: fire orange #F07840, ocean blue #36A9CF, light cyan #62C6EC, ivory text #F2F0EB. Thin flame strokes and wave strokes as line art only, small and restrained, never a big cartoon flame.
- Typography: tall, elegant, widely letter-spaced capitals for headlines (like Tenor Sans), a clean geometric sans for the rest (like Jost). Sentence case for body text.
- Leave a clean empty rectangle, 480 × 220 px, in the top-left corner for the logo; Claude stamps the real logo there afterwards. Do not draw a logo, a panda, or any mascot.
- Avoid: pandas, mascots, cartoon characters, beach cartoons, palm-tree clip art, neon, emoji, watermarks, misspelled words, any words other than the ones listed.

Priority order: 1 to 4 go up on day one. 5 to 7 are nice to have.
