Island Panda is now Fire & Finn.

Same kitchen. Same address. New name. The hibachi, the specialty rolls and the build-your-own poke bowls at 2003 Periwinkle Way carry on as Fire & Finn, grilled hot and rolled cold, open daily 11am to 8pm.

Call to Order See the menu

What changed

  • The name. Island Panda is now Fire & Finn.
  • Nine roll names. The rolls are on the menu under their new names:
    • Island Panda is now Fire & Finn
    • Captiva Sunset is now Golden Hour
    • Hurricane is now Typhoon
    • Pelican is now Kingfisher
    • Periwinkle is now Riptide
    • RIP Tipsy Turtle is now Tipsy Turtle
    • Sand Dollar is now Tide Pool
    • Sanibel Causeway is now Three Tides
    • Lighthouse is now Crazy

What did not

  • The kitchen and the address. 2003 Periwinkle Way, Sanibel, FL 33957, in Tahitian Gardens.
  • The phone. (239) 579-0783.
  • The hours. Open daily, 11am to 8pm.
  • The Tipsy Turtle. Still on the menu, as printed:

    Tipsy Turtle 17
    Krab, avocado, cucumber & cilantro wrapped in salmon, finished with sliced red onion & a touch of lime juice

Where to order

  • Order online, or call (239) 579-0783. Order ahead for pickup.
